WEEK 3 · 12 MIN READ

Mean, median, mode aur spread

Numerical data ko ek number mein summarise karna seekho, aur samjho ki data kitna faila hua hai.

1 · Concept

  • Mean = sab values ka sum ÷ count. Outliers se zyada affect hota hai.
  • Median = sorted data ka middle value. Outliers ka asar kam.
  • Range = max − min. Variance batata hai values mean se kitni door hain.

2 · Solved example

Data: 2, 4, 4, 5, 10
Mean = 25 / 5 = 5  ·  Median = 4  ·  Mode = 4  ·  Range = 8

3 · Common mistake

Median nikalne se pehle data sort karna mat bhoolo. Unsorted list ka middle value median nahi hota.
