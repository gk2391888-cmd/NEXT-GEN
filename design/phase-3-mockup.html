Median aur mean mein outlier ka effect alag kyun hota hai? Example ke saath samjhao.
Short answer: Mean har value ko use karta hai, isliye ek badi value usse kheench leti hai. Median sirf beech wali position dekhta hai.

Step 1: Data lo 2, 4, 6, 8, 100.
Step 2: Mean = 120 / 5 = 24 (100 ne mean ko upar kheench liya).
Step 3: Median = 6 (sorted list ka beech wala number, 100 se fark nahi pada).
